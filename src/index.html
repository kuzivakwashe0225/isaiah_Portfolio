About me

I am Isaiah Kuzivakwashe Chikeya, an AI researcher and software developer with a Computer Systems Engineering degree and an MTech in Data Science & Analytics in progress. I build intelligent software, conduct applied AI research, and promote AI awareness in Zimbabwe.

As an AI Research Assistant at the HIT Centre for Artificial Intelligence, I research automatic speech recognition for low-resource languages and work on large language model integration, AI agents and conversational interfaces. I've also built an AI-enabled crop management system and a smart water management system, both presented at the HIT Technovation Expo 2026.

On the software side, I've been shipping production systems since 2022. At Tuto Consulting I build funeral and life-assurance management systems, delivered the Royal Funerals admin dashboard end to end, and am now building a SaaS dashboard product; at Tetisol I develop software for client companies. My stack centres on React.js, Node.js and Python, with MongoDB, Express, Django and AWS, plus IoT and embedded work such as a Remote Prepaid Water Meter and the Chord Catcher.

Beyond tech, I’m a FIDE-rated chess player, rugby referee under Harare Rugby Referees Society, and an acoustic/lead guitarist. I love blending creativity with logic and am deeply committed to continuous learning and community-driven innovation.

AI Research

At the Centre for Artificial Intelligence, Harare Institute of Technology, my research focuses on making language and speech technology work for communities that mainstream AI tends to leave out — and on turning that research into systems people can actually use.

Automatic Speech Recognition for Low-Resource Languages

Researching and developing ASR models for languages with little transcribed audio and limited existing tooling. The work covers collecting and preparing speech data, adapting pretrained speech models to new languages, and evaluating recognition quality so that voice interfaces become usable in local languages.

Speech recognition · Low-resource NLP · Python · Model fine-tuning

Large Language Models, AI Agents & Conversational Interfaces

Integrating large language models into real applications: building AI agents that can reason over tasks and call tools, and conversational interfaces that let users interact with systems in natural language. Focus on practical, reliable LLM integration rather than demos.

LLM integration · AI agents · Prompt engineering · Node.js / Python

AI-Enabled Crop Management System

Computer vision · Machine learning · Drone imagery

Smart Water Management System
